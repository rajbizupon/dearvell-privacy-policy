Delete your Dearvell account

Dearvell is operated by ZeroSoleil. This page provides an external way to request deletion of a Dearvell account and associated account data. Dearvell is currently in development; the in-app deletion control and operational process must be verified before account-based features are released.

Request account deletion

Email envisionailabs@gmail.com with subject “Delete Dearvell account”. Send it from the email associated with your Dearvell account where possible. Include only your account email and a short statement that you want the account deleted. If you cannot use that email, explain the issue and we will discuss reasonable ownership verification. Never send passwords, purchase tokens, journal entries, encryption keys, or recovery material.

What is removed

After verification, the intended process removes the Dearvell account profile, sign-in associations, sessions, synchronized account preferences, and ordinary account-service records. We aim to complete this within 30 days of a verified request. Minimum records may be retained where necessary for transactions, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal obligations, as explained in the policy.

Local journals and Google Drive are separate

Your writing is not held on Dearvell's application server. Deleting the account does not automatically erase device-local journals, personal Drive backups, or previous exports. You must separately choose whether to remove those copies. Use the app's supported deletion/backup controls or your Google account storage tools. Revoking Drive access alone does not remove existing backups. Retain any copy and recovery material you need before deleting the originals.

Cancel recurring billing separately

If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel in Google Play's subscription settings. An account-deletion email does not automatically cancel the subscription.

No account?

If you only used Dearvell locally without signing in, there is no Dearvell server account to delete. Remove local data through the app/device controls. Check any exports or backups you created separately.

Request account deletion by email

This static website does not send requests automatically. An email link opens your mail app. Please check that your email is actually sent.
