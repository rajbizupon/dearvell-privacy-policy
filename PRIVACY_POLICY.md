# Dearvell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

## Pre-release notice
Dearvell is being developed by ZeroSoleil, operated by Rajvinder Singh. This policy
records the intended data practices for the app's first release. The release build,
third-party SDKs, account-deletion flow, encryption, and retention configuration must
be checked against this policy before Dearvell is made available to users. Features
described here may not yet be available. This page does not claim that an unfinished
feature is implemented or that Dearvell has undergone an independent security audit.

## 1. Who we are and how to contact us
Dearvell is a personal journaling app from ZeroSoleil. For privacy questions,
account deletion, or requests concerning your personal information, contact
envisionailabs@gmail.com with the subject “Dearvell privacy”. Do not send journal
entries, passwords, purchase tokens, encryption keys, or recovery material.

## 2. Your journal and local use
You can use the free local journal without creating a Dearvell account. Journal
entries, journal names, drawings, selected photos, notes, highlights, bookmarks,
and journal appearance settings are saved on your device. Our application backend
is not designed to store or receive the contents of your journals, a per-note
identifier registry, or your plaintext journal encryption or recovery keys.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage can remove device-local writing.
An account alone is not a backup. Save recovery material and complete a backup
before relying on recovery after device loss or replacement.

## 3. Optional accounts and account information
An account is needed for cloud backup and purchases. If you sign in, our services
process an account identifier, authentication-provider identifier, any verified
email supplied by the approved provider, session information, and selected
non-content preferences such as language and default stationery choice.
We use these to authenticate you, provide account services, apply your preferences,
protect sessions, and administer your subscription. Device-only security choices,
such as your biometric preference and notification permission, are not account
preferences that we synchronize to our backend.

## 4. Optional Google Drive backups
If you connect Google Drive, Dearvell prepares encrypted journal backup objects
on your device and uploads them to your Google Drive. This can include encrypted
writing, media, journal settings, and backup manifests needed for restore and
conflict handling. The contents of these encrypted backup objects do not pass
through our application server. Google processes the storage and related
technical metadata under its own terms and privacy policy.

You choose manual backup or scheduled nightly backup. The preferred nightly time
is approximate; connectivity, device restrictions, power state, and other factors
can delay or prevent a run. A backup is complete only after its required objects
and manifest have been uploaded and acknowledged. Unsaved or unbacked-up changes
cannot be recovered from a backup that does not contain them.

When you enable automatic backup, already-prepared encrypted objects may be
uploaded in the background even while the journal interface is locked. This
does not mean that every edit is continuously synchronized. You can change
backup settings or disconnect Drive in the app. You can also revoke access
through your Google account. Revoking access does not itself erase existing
Drive backup files.

## 5. Encryption, app locking, and recovery
Dearvell's intended design encrypts journal backup content before upload and
protects local personal data using the selected device-storage security design.
The app lock controls access to the journal interface. Encryption keys and
recovery material must be protected separately from ordinary preferences.
We do not hold plaintext keys that would let us recover or read your journals.

Cloud backup setup requires recovery material to be saved and confirmed. Account
password recovery is separate from journal decryption recovery. If you lose all
devices with access to the journal and also lose the required recovery material,
we cannot promise that the encrypted writing can be recovered. No storage or
transmission system can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

## 6. Purchases and subscriptions
For purchases made through Google Play, Google handles payment details. Dearvell's
backend processes purchase verification information, product identifiers,
subscription state, and related transaction references to grant access, restore
purchases, handle renewals, and prevent fraud. We do not receive your complete
payment-card details. Purchase proof is not journal content.

Cancelling a subscription and deleting an account are separate actions. Account
deletion does not automatically cancel Google Play billing. Manage or cancel the
subscription through Google Play. After premium access expires, premium journals
remain readable but cannot be edited or exported; the designated free journal
remains usable under free-plan limits. Expiry does not delete writing or make
encryption and backup premium-only.

## 7. Permissions and device features
Dearvell requests permissions only for features you choose to use. These may
include selecting a photo or file, receiving reminders, and using supported
device authentication for an app lock. A system photo/file picker should be
used where possible. Notification permission is optional. Biometric matching is
handled by the device's authentication system; Dearvell is not designed to
receive or store your fingerprint or face template. Dearvell does not require
contacts, call logs, SMS, precise location, or microphone recordings for the
planned journaling features.

## 8. Technical and support information
When you contact or connect to our account services, our infrastructure may
process request timestamps, IP address, app/version and protocol information,
request identifiers, authentication events, error codes, and limited operational
records needed to deliver and protect the service. These records must not
include journal writing, keys, passwords, or recovery material. If you contact
support, we process the contact information and information you choose to send.

The intended release has no advertising SDK, behavioral-advertising profile,
or journal-content analytics. Additional analytics, crash-reporting, or other
SDKs must be reviewed and this policy updated before they are introduced.
We do not sell your personal information or use your journal writing to train
an AI model as part of the described Dearvell service.

## 9. Service providers and disclosure
Information is disclosed only as needed for the features you enable and service
operation: the selected sign-in provider for authentication, Google Drive for
your encrypted backups, Google Play for billing, and contracted hosting and
security providers for limited account and operational data. Exact providers
and SDK behavior must be recorded before release. Providers may process data
in countries other than your own under their applicable terms and safeguards.

We may disclose account/operational information when legally required, to address
fraud or threats, or to protect legitimate rights. This does not give us the
ability to provide plaintext journal content that our servers do not possess.
If the business transfers ownership, affected users should receive notice of
material changes to how their personal data is handled.

## 10. Retention and deletion
Local journals remain on your device until you remove them, clear app storage,
or uninstall the app, subject to platform behavior and your own backups. A
recoverable-delete feature may keep an item until the retention shown in the
app ends. You control copies you export or send to another app.

Account information is retained while needed to provide your account and
services. After an account-deletion request is verified, we aim to remove the
associated account information within 30 days. We may retain the minimum
transaction, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal-compliance records required
for those purposes; retained records are not kept for continued marketing or
ordinary use of a deleted account. Operational logs and protected service
backups must use a finite documented retention schedule. The exact deployment
schedule must be finalized and disclosed before release rather than assumed
to exist today.

Deleting your Dearvell account does not automatically remove copies that remain
on your device, in your personal Drive, or with an export recipient. The app
and the external deletion page must explain these separate choices. Disconnect
Drive and delete its Dearvell backup files through the supported backup controls
or your Google account storage tools. Never delete the only remaining copy
before deciding whether you need to recover it.

## 11. Your choices and requests
You can use the free journal without an account; decline optional permissions;
control automatic backups; disconnect Drive; change selected settings; and
request access, correction, or deletion of account information. Rights under
applicable law are not removed by a subscription restriction. Contact us using
the details above; we may request proportionate account-ownership verification.
Do not provide your journal decryption key as identity verification.

## 12. Account-deletion requests
Use the in-app account-deletion option when available, or visit the Account
deletion page linked from this website. You may email envisionailabs@gmail.com with
subject “Delete Dearvell account” from your account email. If you cannot access
that email, explain this without sending sensitive journal content. We will
confirm the required verification and deletion scope. Cancelling a subscription
alone does not delete the app account; freezing an account is not deletion.

## 13. Children and audience
Dearvell is intended for a general journaling audience and is not designed as a
service directed specifically to children. The final store audience declaration,
age requirements, and any jurisdiction-specific parental-consent obligations
must be checked before release. If you believe a child has provided account
information inappropriately, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed where
required.

## 14. This website
This static policy website does not load advertising, analytics scripts, remote
fonts, or tracking cookies supplied by us. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub
processes requests and associated technical information under its own privacy
statement. Following a third-party link subjects you to that provider's policy.
Email links open your chosen mail application; this static page does not submit
an account-deletion request by itself or prove that an email was delivered.

## 15. Changes and provider policies
We will update the date above when this policy changes. Material changes should
be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel, with consent
where required. Relevant provider policies include Google's Privacy Policy and
GitHub's Privacy Statement. This policy must match the actual release build and
Google Play Data safety disclosures before publication of the app.
